Time-addressable media over Xinsere-protected segments

TAMS keeps the timeline and the query. Xinsere holds, protects and releases the bytes. Each segment below was shredded, per-fragment encrypted and scattered across buckets on the way in — and every frame you play came back out through an authorization check and a bit-perfect integrity verify.

1 · Ingest a flow

Pulls real HLS segments and writes each one through the production pipeline. Give two flows the same timeline to see them align — that's a TAMS Source.

Sizeable segments take a moment — each one is a full store.

2 · The timeline

Every flow is independent — nothing is muxed. Alignment is by shared TAI timestamps, so one instant means the same instant in every lane.

No flows yet. Ingest one above.

3 · Query a timerange

The TAMS lookup: <flow_id, timerange> → Media Objects. What comes back are Xinsere object handles, not storage keys. Nothing is decrypted here — this is the index plane only.

Timestamps are absolute TAI <seconds>:<nanoseconds>, so they are long — use the pickers rather than typing them.

4 · Play the result

Every segment the player fetches goes through the gate: verify, reassemble across buckets, SHA-256, return. Switch to the unauthorized party and playback stops dead — fail-closed returns nothing at all, not a degraded copy.
